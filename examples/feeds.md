# Feeds

RSS and Atom feeds open in-app as a Thread: one card per entry with
author, date, linked title and the entry's HTML (paragraphs, headings,
lists, quotes, code, tables). Images show as `[image: …]` links.

**Ctrl+L** accepts any feed URL. Prefix a page with `feed:` to find its
feed automatically, e.g. `feed:https://jvns.ca/`.
