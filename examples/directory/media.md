## YouTube channels

YouTube publishes an Atom feed per channel: titles, descriptions and links
to the newest videos (playback itself needs a browser: right-click or use an
`external:` link). Find a channel's id in its page source (`channel_id`) or under
*About → Share → Copy channel ID*.

- Science: [NASA](https://www.youtube.com/feeds/videos.xml?channel_id=UCLA_DiR1FfKNvjuUpBHmylQ) · [Kurzgesagt](https://www.youtube.com/feeds/videos.xml?channel_id=UCsXVk37bltHxD1rDPwtNM8Q) · [Veritasium](https://www.youtube.com/feeds/videos.xml?channel_id=UCHnyfMqiRRG1u-2MsSQLbXA) · [SciShow](https://www.youtube.com/feeds/videos.xml?channel_id=UCZYTClx2T1of7BRZ86-8fow) · [PBS Space Time](https://www.youtube.com/feeds/videos.xml?channel_id=UC7_gcs09iThXybpVgjHZ_7g) · [CGP Grey](https://www.youtube.com/feeds/videos.xml?channel_id=UC2C_jShtL725hvbm1arSV9w)
