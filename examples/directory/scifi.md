editorial.

- [Nightmare Magazine](https://www.nightmare-magazine.com/feed/) — dark fantasy and horror
- [Strange Horizons](https://strangehorizons.com/feed/)
- [Beneath Ceaseless Skies](https://www.beneath-ceaseless-skies.com/feed/) — literary adventure fantasy
- [Interzone](https://interzone.press/feed/) — the British SF magazine
- [Asimov's Science Fiction](https://www.asimovs.com/feed/) · [Analog](https://www.analogsf.com/feed/) — latest posts
- [Reactor (formerly Tor.com)](https://reactormag.com/feed/) — fiction, essays, reviews
